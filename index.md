# Bryce Feller - Electrical Engineering Portfolio

I am an Electrical Engineering undergraduate at the University of Minnesota with a focus on electromechanical integration, hardware design, and embedded systems. My engineering approach is driven by hands-on problem solving—from serving as an undergraduate teaching assistant for core circuits courses to developing electrical systems and Python automation tools for the Gopher Motorsports Formula SAE team. 

I am passionate about bridging the gap between theoretical design and physical hardware constraints, utilizing tools like Altium Designer, Autodesk Fusion 360, and LTspice to build robust, real-world solutions.
